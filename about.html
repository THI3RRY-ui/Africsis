About AFRICSIS

The African Center for Science and International Security advances security and strategic stability across Africa through evidence-based analysis, advocacy, and capacity building.

Our Mission

Science for Peace, Security for Progress

AFRICSIS is an independent, non-profit organization dedicated to advancing security and strategic stability across Africa through evidence-based analysis, advocacy, and capacity building. Founded in 2018, we work at the intersection of science, technology, and international security.

We believe complex scientific and security topics — from nuclear safeguards to climate risk — should be understandable to policymakers, credible to international partners, and grounded in rigorous, independent research.

Evidence-Based

Independent

Africa-Led

Regional Footprint

Where We Work

AFRICSIS is headquartered in Accra, Ghana, with active program engagement across West and Central Africa — delivering training, research partnerships, and policy dialogue directly in the countries we serve.
