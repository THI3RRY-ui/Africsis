Chemical & Biological Threats

Supporting Chemical Weapons Convention and Biological Weapons Convention implementation, and strengthening national biosecurity preparedness across the continent.

Overview

Closing Implementation Gaps

Many African signatory states face real gaps between what the Chemical Weapons Convention and Biological Weapons Convention require and what national systems can currently deliver. This program identifies those gaps precisely and builds the technical and legislative capacity to close them.

We work with health, agriculture, and security ministries to strengthen biosecurity governance and oversight of dual-use research, treating chemical and biological threats as a shared institutional responsibility rather than a purely military one.

Focus Areas

Where We Concentrate Our Work

CWC Implementation Support

Technical assistance to close national implementation gaps under the Chemical Weapons Convention.

Biosecurity Preparedness

Minimum preparedness standards and governance frameworks for national biosecurity systems.

Dual-Use Research Oversight

Frameworks to review and manage research with legitimate scientific and misuse potential.

National Legislative Frameworks

Drafting support for legislation that aligns domestic law with treaty obligations.
